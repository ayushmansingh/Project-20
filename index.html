✈

By air

Most guests fly into Delhi (DEL) — the largest hub, with the best domestic and international links — then take one of the ~3-hour trains above, or drive down (~5½ hrs).

Landing closer? Gwalior (GWL) has a brand-new terminal with daily non-stops from Delhi & Mumbai (plus Indore and Bhopal), just ~10 km from town.

Search flights ↗
❀

By road

From Delhi (~355 km, 5½–6 hrs): the Yamuna Expressway to Agra, then NH-44 straight down. From Agra it's ~120 km (~2 hrs) — an easy, scenic drive.

Self-drive or hire an outstation taxi; the resort has parking, and we're glad to help arrange a cab.
